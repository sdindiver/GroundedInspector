Add 1-3 exemplar images of EMBOSSING_MISSING for the BRACKET here (part defect).
embossing_missing = the required "VA" embossed marking is ABSENT where it belongs
on the FRONT face. The assembler globs *.jpg/*.png in this folder; filenames free.

*** CRITICAL - the exemplar MUST be a genuine FRONT face with the VA absent ***
A blank VA zone is NORMAL on the BACK face - it is NOT this defect. If you drop a
back-face photo here, the API will learn to FALSE-FLAG good back-facing parts.
So the image you add MUST show the FRONT face by its edge/hole profile:
  - FRONT (correct for this defect): a CHAMFERED / ROLLED-OVER bevel band runs
    around the whole perimeter and both rounded ends, AND the two plain holes are
    COUNTERSUNK (sit in shallow dished depressions) - yet the VA zone is BLANK.
  - BACK (do NOT use): outer edge is SQUARE and SHARP with no bevel, plain holes
    punch through FLAT. A blank VA here is normal, not a defect.
The VA zone is between the MIDDLE hole and the plain end-hole, on the side of the
middle hole OPPOSITE the serrated hole. Distinguish from incomplete_embossing
(VA present but one character shallow/faint).
